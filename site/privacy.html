Privacy Policy

Ishvara (Android app com.ishvara.sadhana) · Last updated: 19 September 2026

Ishvara is a devotional app for watching videos and listening to songs. It has no accounts, no sign-up, no ads, and no in-app purchases. This policy explains the small amount of data involved in running it.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or any device identifiers. You never create a profile.

Information stored only on your phone

  • Journey progress: the dates you completed the daily practice, which is used to show your streak.
  • Reflections: any optional notes you type during the daily practice.
  • Content cache: a copy of the video and song list so the app opens quickly.

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it, and you can also erase your progress in Settings → Reset Journey progress.

Ask Divya (AI guide)

If you use Ask Divya, the text of your question is sent to our server (hosted on Supabase) and then to Google’s Gemini API to generate an answer. We do not store your questions or the answers. Google processes this text under its Gemini API terms. Please don’t include personal information in your questions.

To prevent abuse, our server temporarily records your IP address and the time of each question. These records are deleted within 24 hours.

Answers are AI-generated and may contain mistakes. They are not medical, psychological, legal, or financial advice.

Streaming videos and songs

Videos and songs are streamed from our content delivery network, Bunny.net. Like any web server, it receives your IP address and basic request information in order to deliver the media, and it may keep standard access logs for a short period. See Bunny.net’s privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data. When you tap Share, the text you share goes to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp), under that app’s own policies.

Children

Ishvara is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

All network traffic uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
